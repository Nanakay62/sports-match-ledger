# Editorial Policy: What Not to Monetise

## Purpose
The product thesis of Sports News AI is: **"The sports news app that keeps the receipts."** 
The Accountability Ledger exists to build public trust through tamper-proof attribution and verified outcomes. Compromising editorial independence for short-term ad or placement revenue would destroy the core value proposition.

This document codifies the non-negotiable boundaries governing platform monetisation.

---

## The Prohibited Monetisation List

### 1. No Paid Placement in Feeds
* Stories, claims, and event cards are ranked strictly using deterministic, rule-based algorithms (recency, corroboration depth, authority rank).
* No publisher, agent, club, or sponsor may pay to promote, pin, or artificially boost a story or claim in any reader feed.

### 2. No Sponsored "Verified" or "Reliable" Badges
* Reliability scores (Wilson score lower bounds) and status badges (`Confirmed`, `Well corroborated`, etc.) are computed mathematically from empirical outcomes on the append-only ledger.
* Badges cannot be purchased, expedited, or altered by commercial agreements.

### 3. No Selling User Reading Behaviour or Personal Data
* We do not sell, rent, or broker reader telemetry, search queries, or watchlist data to third-party ad networks, data brokers, or gambling syndicates.
* User entitlements and watchlist data are used solely for service delivery and local personalization.

### 4. No Full Article Paywalling / Republishing
* The current status and verified evidence bullets of every tracked story are **free forever**.
* We never paywall the core news answer to "what's the latest?". Pro subscriptions only gate deep archival history, real-time alert webhooks, and raw data export.
* We never reproduce full external publisher articles behind our paywall; we publish only transformative, fact-based summaries with prominent canonical links back to original sources.

### 5. No Betting Tips, Predictions, or Gambling Affiliates
* The platform does not offer betting advice, speculative outcome probabilities, odds comparisons, or tipster content.
* Gambling affiliate revenue (CPA, revenue-share links to sportsbooks) is **explicitly excluded** from the base revenue model pending jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

---

## Enforcement & Review
Any change to this policy requires unanimous approval from editorial and technical leadership and must be published to readers with at least 30 days notice.
