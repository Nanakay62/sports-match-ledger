# ADR-0010: Deterministic Validation Statuses and Independent Evidence Roots

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Handbook §14.2 is explicit that the reader-facing status must never be an opaque truth percentage ("87% true" invites an argument no one can win) — it must be one of a fixed, explainable vocabulary, computed from named factors a reader could audit themselves. Getting the *independence* of corroborating sources wrong is the single most common aggregator failure mode the handbook calls out (§1.2, §12.4): five sites republishing one wire story is not five confirmations, it is one.

## Decision
`DeterministicValidationEngine` (`packages/common/validation.py`) assigns exactly the six statuses the handbook names — `RUMOUR`, `DEVELOPING`, `WELL_CORROBORATED`, `CONFIRMED`, `DISPUTED`, `CORRECTED` (`EventStatus`, `packages/common/models.py`) — from deterministic factors: source quality, independent corroboration count, capped reporter-record weight, consistency across claims, official confirmation, contradiction detection, and recency. No status is ever generated by a model call. Independence is computed upstream in `workers/pipeline/attribution.py`, which detects syndication and repetition patterns (`REPETITION_CUE_PATTERNS`, `SYNDICATION_PATTERNS` — "according to", "cites", "Source:", wire-service reporting-credit lines) so that the evidence-root count reflects genuinely independent origins, not raw article count. A denial and an assertion about the same event are never silently merged into one status; they produce a `DISPUTED` status with both sides visible (tested in `tests/unit/test_cross_feed_dedup.py`).

## Consequences
- **Positive**: the status shown to a reader is always reconstructable from a fixed rule set, which is what makes the "why this status?" disclosure panel (`apps/web/components/why-status-panel.tsx`) possible and honest — there is a real, inspectable reason behind every label, never a black-box confidence score.
- **Negative**: because independence detection is regex/heuristic-based rather than a full citation graph, a syndication pattern the patterns don't recognise (an unusual attribution phrasing, a new wire service) could be miscounted as an independent root until the pattern list is extended — this is a known precision/recall trade-off of a deterministic-only approach, accepted deliberately (per ADR-0005) over the alternative of asking a model to judge independence, which would reintroduce exactly the ungrounded-inference risk the ledger exists to avoid.
